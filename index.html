WELCOME TO EMBER & OAK

Crafted by Fire, Guided by Seasonality

At EMBER & OAK, every evening unfolds around the primordial warmth of our custom white-oak and cherrywood hearth.

Led by Executive Chef Julian Vance, our kitchen partners directly with Hudson Valley farms, coastal day-boat fishermen, and artisanal ranchers. Each dish balances smoke, acidity, and delicate texture—paired effortlessly with rare Old World vintages and botanical cocktails.

CHEF'S SIGNATURE TABLE PRESENTATION

Côte de Boeuf Over Live White Oak

Our signature 36-ounce bone-in prime rib roast is dry-aged in-house for 45 days on Himalayan salt blocks, gently warmed beside the masonry hearth, then seared directly over glowing white-oak coals.

  • Accompaniments: Roasted bone marrow, pomme purée, charred baby leeks
  • Sommelier Pairing: 2018 Pauillac Reserve or Côte-Rôtie Syrah

OUR STORY & PROVENANCE

Rooted in Patience, Smoke & Integrity

Since opening our doors on Mercer Street in 2008, EMBER & OAK has remained committed to a singular ethos: zero gas burners, 100% seasoned hardwood embers, and unhurried hospitality.

Head Sommelier Clara Mercer curates a 380-label subterranean cellar focused on sustainable growers, library Barolos, and rare Champagne houses designed to harmonize with subtle hearth smoke.

100% White Oak & Cherry

Kiln-cured orchard and Appalachian timber for clean, aromatic heat.

12 Hudson Valley Farms

Daily harvests of heirloom roots, pasture poultry, and raw-milk cheeses.
